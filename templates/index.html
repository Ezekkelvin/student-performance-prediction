ONE CONNECTED WORKFLOW

Everything works together.

From class registration to early intervention, each role has the tools it needs to contribute to the process.

▦ 01 · ATTENDANCE

Keep reliable class records

Lecturers schedule sessions, manage registers and review student attendance claims. Confirmed records contribute to each course's attendance percentage.

◎ 02 · PREDICTION

Spot potential risk earlier

Class advisors combine attendance with assignment, practical, CA, previous CGPA and class participation indicators to generate a prediction.

↗ 03 · INTERVENTION

Turn insight into action

When a student is classified as At Risk, an advisor can choose to email the lecturer assigned to that course for follow-up support.
