WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

One platform, four essential capabilities.

Connect classroom activity, academic records, prediction, and intervention in one organized workflow.

01

Attendance management

Lecturers schedule classes, open attendance registers, review student claims, and finalize each session.

02

Performance prediction

The trained machine learning model evaluates six pre-exam features to estimate a student's performance category.

03

Academic monitoring

Class Advisors select an enrolled student and course, then review the relevant academic indicators alongside calculated attendance.

04

Early intervention

When a current prediction is At Risk, a Class Advisor can choose to notify the lecturer assigned to that course by email.

THE PREDICTION MODEL

Six inputs. One consistent feature order.

The prediction uses attendance and academic indicators that are available before final examinations. Student identity and level help identify the record but are not inputs to the model.

HOW IT WORKS

From classroom activity to timely support.

Each role contributes to a clear process, with permissions that limit users to the tasks they are allowed to perform.

1

Record attendance

Lecturers schedule sessions and manage attendance registers for their assigned courses.

2

Review academic indicators

The Class Advisor selects a student and enrolled course and supplies or verifies the available academic scores.

3

Generate a prediction

The model combines the six features and returns a performance category and its prediction confidence.

4

Consider intervention

For an At Risk result, the Class Advisor can initiate an email alert to the course lecturer.

RESPONSIBLE USE

A prediction is a signal, not a final judgement.

Model outputs should support academic review and timely assistance, not replace a lecturer's or advisor's professional judgement. Prediction confidence is not a guarantee of a student's eventual result.
